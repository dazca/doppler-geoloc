# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Curious people and OSINT hobbyists visiting azemar.eu (an XP-desktop-style personal site). They have a video or audio clip with small planes or helicopters audible overhead and want to know where it was filmed. Mostly non-experts in signal processing; some OSINT practitioners who want to inspect and correct each step.

## Product Purpose
Geolocate a recording from the Doppler sound of passing aircraft, matched against real ADS-B tracks, entirely in the browser. Success: drop a file, press one button, get a map pin with an honest verdict (match / weak / no plausible match); experts can open each step and edit it.

## Brand Personality
Precise, quiet, honest. A lab instrument, not a marketing page. Never claims more confidence than the evidence supports.

## Anti-references
Paragraph-heavy research demos; dashboards full of sliders shown up front; "AI magic" hype; confident results without uncertainty.

## Design Principles
- Automatic first, editable always: one primary action; every automatic decision can be opened and corrected.
- Show, don't tell: the spectrogram, curves and map carry the explanation; text is a short hint or a (?) away.
- Progressive disclosure: advanced settings hidden until asked for.
- Honest output: verdict and uncertainty are always visible next to the pin.
- Bilingual: Catalan default, English toggle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, keyboard operable (spectrogram editing has keyboard/button alternatives), help tooltips reachable by focus, reduced-motion respected.
